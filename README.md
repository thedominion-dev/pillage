# Pillage

Turn your streams into ready-to-post clips. Pillage finds the best moments in your Twitch VODs or your own video files, cuts them to vertical or horizontal, and burns in captions. Windows only. It runs on your own PC.

**Get it:** [getpillage.com](https://getpillage.com) (free Lite) | [Pillage Pro, $39.99 one time](https://fanblade.gumroad.com/l/oiflci)

## Clip styles

Horizontal, Crop, Blur, and Facecam (Pro).
